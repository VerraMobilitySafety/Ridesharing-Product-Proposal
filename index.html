The problem

The safety challenge isn't detection. It's connection.

A single incident can involve the driver, rider, app, support team, safety team, insurer, emergency services and regulator. The hard part is connecting them quickly, consistently and with the right evidence.

A serious safety risk shouldn't disappear just because someone changes apps.

An independently governed Industry Safety Register.

Not a blacklist. A tightly controlled framework for substantiated serious safety events or exclusions, with verified identity, evidence thresholds, appeals, access controls and regulator oversight.

Already possible

Still missing
