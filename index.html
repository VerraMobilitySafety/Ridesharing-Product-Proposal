The opportunity

The safety challenge isn't detection. It's connection.

A single mobility incident can involve the driver, rider, app, customer support, internal safety, insurance, emergency services and regulators. The opportunity is to connect that entire lifecycle through one human-led safety operating layer.

Industry safety intelligence

A serious safety risk shouldn't disappear because someone changes platforms.

Industry Safety Register

Explore a tightly governed, central safety intelligence capability for substantiated serious events or exclusions — accessible only to authorised platforms and relevant government bodies.

Not a blacklist. Identity verification, defined evidence thresholds, appeals, privacy, access controls and independent oversight would be fundamental.
